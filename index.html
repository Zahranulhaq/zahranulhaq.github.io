About

Hello — I'm Zahran Ul Haq. I'm a freelance designer & artist working on personal projects and client work since 2021.

My practice blends AI-assisted design and prompt engineering with hands-on craft in CorelDRAW and Canva — logos, brand marks, social media visuals, photo editing, and video edits. I believe in bold ideas, striking color, and designs that get seen.

This site is a living sketchbook — a selection of projects and experiments from the past few years.
